# BrokenFront Wiki

BrokenFront is OpenFront plus an expansion: a uranium economy, two new
warheads, an offshore economy, an air war, international trade and new ways to
fight alongside your allies.

This wiki describes each feature: what it is, how to build or use it, and the
numbers behind it. The **Help** page covers the classic game — spawning,
attacking, cities, ports, factories, warships, silos and the original nukes.

## Which features are on?

Every expansion feature is a toggle on the lobby config, so a game can run
anywhere between the classic ruleset and the full expansion:

| Toggle          | Covers                                                                      |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `uranium`       | Uranium deposits, Uranium Mines, uranium costs on nukes, the uranium market |
| `airWar`        | Airbases, Aircraft Carriers, Fighters, Bombers, paradrops                   |
| `seaEconomy`    | Oil Rigs and Fishing Boats                                                  |
| `tradeDeals`    | Trade agreements, tariffs and player-to-player offers                       |
| `alliedTransit` | Marching troops through allied land                                         |

Unless a lobby says otherwise, all five are **on** in singleplayer and private
games and **off** in public games, which stay on the classic ruleset until the
expansion is balanced for them. The in-game tutorial skips the steps for any
feature a game has turned off.

## Keeping this up to date

This wiki is part of the game, not a separate site: the pages live in
`resources/wiki/` in the repository. When a feature changes, the page that
describes it and the in-game tutorial step that teaches it change with it.
