# Uranium

Uranium is the second resource. Gold buys buildings; uranium arms warheads.
Your stockpile sits next to your gold in the control panel whenever the
uranium economy is on.

## Deposits

Uranium sits in fixed circular deposits — a handful of tiles across — scattered
over inland plains and highland. They are **the same in every game on the same
map**, so the good ground is worth learning, and every player sees the same set.

Deposits are hidden until you look for them: pick **Uranium Mine** in the build
menu and the deposits light up as circles on the map. Larger maps get more
deposits (roughly one per 60,000 land tiles, between 4 and 48).

## Uranium Mine

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | 400k gold, rising 400k per mine, capped at 1.5M |
| Build time | 15s |
| Placement | On a uranium deposit inside your territory |
| Output | 1 uranium per level every 6s |
| Upgradable | Yes — each level adds another 1 uranium per cycle |

A mine must stand on a deposit, so taking and holding deposit ground is the
whole uranium game. Mines are ordinary structures otherwise: they can be
bombed, nuked, and captured with the land under them.

## What uranium is for

Nukes cost uranium on top of their gold price:

| Warhead | Gold | Uranium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atom Bomb | 750k | 10 |
| Large Nuke | 2.5M | 25 |
| Cluster Nuke | 2.25M | 30 |
| Hydrogen Bomb | 5M | 40 |
| MIRV | 25M (+15M per MIRV already launched) | 150 |

If a lobby disables Uranium Mines there would be no way to earn uranium, so
uranium costs switch off with them.

## Getting uranium without mining

- **The world market.** The trade panel buys and sells uranium for gold at a
  price that moves with demand. See [Trade](#modal=wiki&page=trade).
- **Offers.** Swap gold and uranium with any player you can trade with, or send
  a one-sided offer as a gift to an ally. See
  [Fighting with allies](#modal=wiki&page=allies).
- **Conquest.** Take the deposit and build your own mine on it.
