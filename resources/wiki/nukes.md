# Nukes

BrokenFront adds two warheads between the Atom Bomb and the Hydrogen Bomb.
Both launch from a Missile Silo like the classic nukes: pick the warhead, click
the target, and the nearest ready silo fires. Both cost uranium
([Uranium](#modal=wiki&page=uranium)).

## Large Nuke

|       |                                                          |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cost  | 2.5M gold + 25 uranium                                   |
| Blast | 40 tiles of total destruction, out to 60 tiles of damage |
| Silo  | Any finished silo that isn't reloading                   |

A bigger Atom Bomb. Where the atom bomb opens a hole in a border, the Large
Nuke takes out a city cluster, and it reaches far enough to hit the buildings
behind the front line. Cheaper per tile than a Hydrogen Bomb, with a blast
small enough to use next to your own land.

## Cluster Nuke

|          |                                                   |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Cost     | 2.25M gold + 30 uranium                           |
| Warheads | 5 atom-sized warheads                             |
| Pattern  | One on the aim point, four on a ring 40 tiles out |
| Silo     | Level 2 or higher                                 |

Five atom bombs for the price of three — but spread, not stacked. It flies as a
single carrier missile and splits near the target, so **shooting the carrier
down before it splits stops the whole salvo**: one SAM hit cancels five
warheads. It is the weapon for a wide front, and the wrong weapon for a hard
single target.

## Defending against them

- **SAM Launchers** intercept any incoming missile in range (70 tiles at level
  1, approaching 150 at high level), with a 7.5s cooldown. A cluster nuke
  caught before it splits is five warheads stopped at once.
- **Fighters** shoot down bombers and transport planes, not missiles — see
  [Air war](#modal=wiki&page=air-war).
- **Spread out.** The Cluster Nuke punishes long, dense borders; the Large Nuke
  punishes stacked buildings.

## Diplomacy

Nuking a player you are allied with breaks the alliance if the blast covers
more than about 100 of their tiles or catches any of their structures — and a
cluster nuke is checked against the footprint of the whole salvo, not one
warhead, so it is very easy to anger a neighbour with. Breaking an alliance
this way marks you a traitor like any other betrayal.
