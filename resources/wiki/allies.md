# Fighting with allies

Alliances used to mean "we don't attack each other" plus gold and troop
donations. BrokenFront adds three ways to actually fight a war together.

## Allied transit — "March via allies"

Right-click a target and pick **March via allies**. Your troops form a column
that marches **through allied territory** and attacks the target from the
allied side of its border, establishing a bridgehead exactly like a boat
landing.

- The corridor must stay friendly the whole way. If an alliance breaks, or the
  land changes hands under the column, it turns back and the troops come home
  with the usual retreat penalty.
- It is the land answer to the problem of being walled in by your own friends:
  you no longer have to go around an ally's territory, or sail past it, to
  reach the enemy behind it.
- A column is visible and slow. Marching across a long alliance is a
  commitment, not a surprise.

## Call to arms

Right-click a hostile player and pick **Call to arms**. Every ally gets a
message naming you and the target, and the target is marked as yours. AI allies
that like you well enough (Friendly or better) will target the enemy
themselves, and the target starts to dislike them for it.

There is a **30s cooldown** per player, and you need at least one ally and a
hostile target. It is the fastest way to turn a one-on-one war into a pile-on.

## Sharing uranium

Gold and troops are donated from the radial menu as always. Uranium moves
through the **Trade** panel instead: send an offer that gives uranium and asks
for nothing, and your ally has 30 seconds to accept it. The recipient gets a
message naming you.

That is how a team shares a warhead: whoever holds the deposits mines, and
whoever holds the silos launches. See [Uranium](#modal=wiki&page=uranium) and
[Trade](#modal=wiki&page=trade).

## Betrayal still costs

None of this changes the price of treachery: break an alliance and you're a
traitor for 30 seconds with a heavy defence debuff, trade with that player
stops for 5 minutes, and both tribes and players remember it.
