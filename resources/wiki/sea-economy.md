# Sea economy

Ports already earn gold from trade ships. The sea economy adds two ways to earn
from water you control, neither of which depends on anyone trading with you —
so they keep paying while you are at war and under embargo.

## Oil Rig

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | 600k gold, rising 600k per rig, capped at 2M |
| Build time | 10s |
| Health | 600 |
| Placement | Open water within 10 tiles of your own land |
| Income | 8,000 gold per level every 5s |
| Upgradable | Yes |

Offshore and undefended: a rig has no guns, and sits in water where enemy
warships and bombers can reach it. It also needs a supply line — a rig whose
owner no longer holds land within 12 tiles is **abandoned and destroyed**, so
losing the coast behind a rig loses the rig.

Rigs are the most reliable gold in the game for a player with a quiet coastline,
and the easiest money to raid for a player with a navy.

## Fishing Boats

Fishing boats are free and automatic. Each port keeps **one boat per port level**
at sea: every 20s a port with a free slot sends one out, it sails 25–60 tiles
across the same body of water, fishes for 15s, and brings the catch home.

The catch pays the port's owner `6,000 + 80 × distance` gold, so distant
fishing grounds are worth more. Fishing is not trade: **embargoes don't stop
it**, and neither does being at war. Enemy warships sink the boats, which is
the only way to stop it.

## In practice

- A coastal player with upgraded ports earns a steady income nobody can
  embargo away.
- A landlocked player can't touch either feature — water access matters more
  in BrokenFront than in the classic game.
- Warships now have targets worth patrolling for beyond trade ships.
